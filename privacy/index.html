1. Introduction

Feltzee respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your data is handled when using the Feltzee app.

2. Data that stays on your device

By default, Feltzee does not collect, store, or share personal data on a Feltzee server. There is no Feltzee backend.

The following stay on your device unless you turn on Game Center (see below):

  • The in-progress game
  • Settings (default player name, dice color, celebrations)
  • The local High Scores list (names you type, scores, dates)

Those local high-score names are not sent to Game Center. Game Center uses your Game Center nickname, not the name in Settings.

3. Optional Game Center

If you turn on Post scores to Game Center in Settings and complete Apple’s Game Center sign-in, Feltzee posts each finished game’s total to Apple Game Center (Daily and All Time leaderboards).

Apple, not Feltzee, stores that data. Posted scores use your Game Center nickname. Nicknames and scores on those boards are visible to other Game Center players.

Turning the switch off stops new posts. It does not remove scores already on Game Center. To manage or delete Game Center data, use your Apple ID, iOS Settings → Game Center, and Apple’s privacy tools.

If you leave the switch off, Feltzee does not sign in to Game Center and does not post scores.

4. No analytics or tracking

Feltzee does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking. It does not use App Tracking Transparency. It does not create a Feltzee account or use Sign in with Apple of its own.

5. Changes to this Privacy Policy

If this policy changes, the latest version will be at the URL above and linked from Settings in the app.
