## 6. Keyword research

Feltzee is a Yahtzee-style game, so nearly all search demand lives on "yahtzee" and
"dice game" terms. Two funnels matter:

- **Transactional** ("yahtzee app no ads", "offline dice game") — small volume, extremely
  high download intent. Target on the homepage + comparison-style guides.
- **Informational** ("how to play yahtzee", "what is a good yahtzee score") — large volume,
  lower intent, but each answer naturally demonstrates a Feltzee feature (auto-scoring,
  joker handling, high-score tracking). Target with guide pages that end in a download CTA.

Trademark note: "Yahtzee" is a Hasbro trademark. Use it nominatively/informationally
("classic Yahtzee rules", "a Yahtzee-style dice game") and keep a disclaimer in the footer.
Never call the app "Yahtzee" as its name.

### Rules facts used in guides (verified against Hasbro's official rules)

- Upper bonus: 35 points when the upper section totals 63+ (three of each number).
- Full house: three of one number + two of another = 25 points.
- Small straight: any sequence of four = 30 points. Large straight: sequence of five = 40.
- Five of a kind (Yahtzee): 50 points. Each additional one after scoring 50: +100 bonus,
  then place the roll by the joker rules (upper box for that number first; if filled, any
  open lower box at its fixed value; if all filled, zero an upper box).
- A zero in the five-of-a-kind box means no 100-point bonuses later, but joker rules still apply.
- Maximum possible score: 1,575 (with 13 straight five-of-a-kinds). A good casual score is
  250+; the ~35-point bonus swing usually decides it; average solo scores land near 250
  with optimal play, lower casually.
