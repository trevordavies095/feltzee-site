Classic five-dice scoring on a green felt table.

Feltzee is offline five-dice scoring on iPhone. The whole game stays on one portrait screen. There is no account and no internet required to play. Pass the phone. Beat the house high score.

What you get

  • Classic rules, including bonus five-of-a-kind and joker placement
  • Tap to hold, with potential scores on every open box
  • In-progress games saved on device
  • Shared top-10 high scores with editable names
  • Optional Game Center Daily and All Time leaderboards
  • Haptics only, no sound. Sign-in only if you opt into Game Center
  • Green felt in Light and Dark
