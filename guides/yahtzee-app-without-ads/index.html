Finding a Yahtzee-Style App Without Ads

Most free dice apps monetize with interstitial ads between turns and paywalled features. Feltzee is a free Yahtzee-style game with no ads, no in-app purchases required to play, no account, and no data collection. Unlocks are earned by scoring, not paying.

The ad problem in dice games

The classic five-dice game has turns that last about fifteen seconds, which makes it perfect ad inventory. Typical free apps insert a 30-second interstitial every two or three turns, sell an ad-removal purchase, and paywall dice skins behind coin bundles. A 13-turn game ends up longer on ads than on dice.

What “ad-free” should actually mean

  • No interstitials or banners: not “fewer,” none.
  • No ad-removal purchase: if removing ads costs money, the free experience is the ad experience.
  • No tracking. Ad-free usually means no ad SDKs, which is why the privacy label is worth reading.
  • Progression without payment. Cosmetics and modes earned by playing, not bought.

Why do most free dice apps have so many ads?

Short turns create constant natural break points, which is exactly where ad networks pay to insert interstitials. Ad-free games have to be built that way on purpose.
