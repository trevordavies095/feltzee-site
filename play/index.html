Play Feltzee

Feltzee is offline five-dice scoring on iPhone. The whole game stays on one portrait screen. There is no account and no internet required to play. A daily puzzle plus other ways to play the same 13-box card.

How a turn works

Roll up to three times a turn. Hold the dice you want. Fill thirteen boxes, Aces through Chance, full house, straights, and five of a kind.

What you get

  • Classic rules, including bonus five-of-a-kind and joker placement
  • Six modes: Today's Dice, Classic, House, Down, Call, and Hand
  • Tap to hold, with potential scores on every open box
  • In-progress games saved on device
  • Optional Game Center high scores when you opt in
  • Ember and Forge unlock from a Today's Dice streak of 7 and 30
  • Haptics only, no sound. Sign-in only if you opt into Game Center
  • Green felt in Light and Dark

Feltzee is a quiet table in your pocket. Pass the phone. Play Today's Dice or another mode on the same card.
