How It Works

Roll. Hold. Score. Thirteen boxes to fill.

Classic five-dice rules, exactly as you remember them, including the upper bonus, extra five-of-a-kind bonuses, and joker placement.

1

Roll up to three times

Each turn starts with a fresh roll of five dice. You get up to two re-rolls to chase the combination you want.

2

Tap to hold your dice

Hold the keepers between rolls. Feltzee shows the potential score in every open box, so you always know your best move.

3

Fill the scorecard

Score each turn in one of 13 boxes: Aces through Chance, full house, straights, and five of a kind. Chase the 35-point upper bonus and beat the house high score.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How do you play a Yahtzee-style dice game?

Roll five dice up to three times per turn, holding any dice between rolls. After your last roll, score the dice in one of 13 boxes: Aces through Sixes on top, combinations like full house, straights, and five of a kind below. The game ends when every box is filled.

Learn more: How to Play Yahtzee →

What are the 13 boxes on a Yahtzee score sheet?

Six upper boxes (Aces through Sixes) score the total of matching dice. Seven lower boxes cover 3 of a Kind, 4 of a Kind, Full House (25), Small Straight (30), Large Straight (40), 5 of a Kind (50), and Chance.

Learn more: The Score Sheet, Explained →

What is a good Yahtzee score?

Over 250 is a good casual score, 300+ is excellent, and 400+ usually means multiple five-of-a-kinds. The theoretical maximum is 1,575. The 35-point upper bonus is the swing that decides most games.

Learn more: What Is a Good Score? →

What happens if you roll a second five of a kind?

If you already scored 50 in the five-of-a-kind box, each extra one is worth a 100-point bonus, and then the joker rules decide where the roll is placed. Feltzee applies both automatically.

Learn more: Bonus & Joker Rules →

How many points is a full house?

A full house (three of one number plus two of another) is always 25 points, regardless of which numbers show.

Learn more: Full House Rules →

What's the difference between a small and large straight?

A small straight is any run of four numbers and scores 30. A large straight is a run of all five dice and scores 40.

Learn more: Small vs. Large Straight →

How do you get the 35-point upper bonus?

Score 63 or more across the six upper boxes, the equivalent of three of each number. Feltzee shows your running progress toward 63 right on the scorecard.

Learn more: The Upper Section Bonus →

What's the best strategy to win?

Bank Fives and Sixes early for the upper bonus, keep Chance as a safety net, take zeros in cheap boxes instead of wasting a good roll, and always chase five of a kind when three matching dice show up early.

Learn more: Strategy Guide →

Can you play Yahtzee by yourself?

Yes. Solo play is you against your own best score. Feltzee saves games mid-turn, keeps a local top 10, and offers optional Game Center Daily and All Time leaderboards.

Learn more: Playing Solo →

Does Feltzee work offline, without wifi?

Yes. No internet is required to play: flights, subways, and dead zones are fine. Game Center leaderboards are the only optional online feature.

Learn more: Offline Dice Games →

Does Feltzee have ads or in-app purchases?

No ads, no paywalls, no account, and no data collected. New felt tables unlock by scoring higher, not by paying.

Can my family share one phone to play?

Yes. Feltzee was built for a shared family phone. Finished games save to a local top 10 with each player's name, so you can pass the phone around the table.
